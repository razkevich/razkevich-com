About

I’m Alex. I work on infrastructure and write about the parts of systems that tend to go wrong in production — reliability patterns, data stores, messaging, Kubernetes, and the trade-offs behind everyday architectural choices.

These notes are mostly things I wanted a clearer mental model for: isolation and failure domains, consistency, tenancy, search, cost, and the operational details that don’t show up in the happy-path diagram.

If something here is wrong or incomplete, I’m glad to hear it.
